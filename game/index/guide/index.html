JEEP HORROR Complete Survival Guide, Fare Mechanics & Entity Lore

The definitive survival wiki for JEEP HORROR. Master day-1 routes, dashboard fare calculations, monster identification, and all secret endings in one comprehensive guide.

Quick Answer: To survive in JEEP HORROR, balance steady vehicle control with sharp cabin vigilance. Always glance at your overhead rear-view mirror when passengers board, calculate change swiftly on your mechanical calculator (standard fare: 13 PHP, student/senior: 10 PHP) within 6 seconds, and inspect commuters for anatomical anomalies, missing reflections, or unblinking stares to identify and outsmart deadly doppelgangers.

1. Key Controls & Pre-Shift Preparation

Action Control Key Strategic Purpose

Before pressing the gas on night one, familiarize yourself with your dashboard layout: the mechanical calculator, the coin sorting tray, and your mirror sightlines. Keeping your eyes moving between the windshield and the passenger cabin is the single most vital habit in JEEP HORROR.

2. Day 1 Walkthrough & Passenger Protocol

Day 1 introduces the core mechanics and pacing of your midnight shift:

  1. Starting the Route: Disengage the handbrake (Spacebar) and turn on low-beam headlights. Accelerate cautiously through the first district.
  2. Passenger Pickup: Commuters wait under illuminated streetlights or near convenience stores. Decelerate smoothly and halt directly inside the pickup zone.
  3. Handling the First Exchange: A regular worker will board and hand forward a 50-peso banknote. Compute the standard 13-peso fare, dispense 37 pesos in change from your coin organizer, and proceed without delay.
  4. Spotting the First Anomaly: Near the midpoint of the route, streetlamps will flicker. If a passenger sits frozen in the back row and refuses to respond when you announce the next stop, do not stare directly at their face for more than two seconds. Continue driving steadily to the terminal.

3. Fare Calculator & Change Mechanics

Accurate cash handling keeps normal passengers satisfied and prevents aggressive outbursts:

Base Fare Pricing Table

  • Regular Commuter: 13 Philippine Pesos (PHP)
  • Discount Brackets (Students, Seniors, PWD): 10 Philippine Pesos (PHP)
  • Common Bill Exchanges:
    • From a 20 PHP bill: Return 7 PHP (regular) or 10 PHP (discount).
    • From a 50 PHP bill: Return 37 PHP (regular) or 40 PHP (discount).
    • From a 100 PHP bill: Return 87 PHP (regular) or 90 PHP (discount).

Operating the Mechanical Calculator

  • Always hit C (Clear) before starting a new calculation.
  • Select coins from the dashboard tray in descending order: 20 PHP, 10 PHP, 5 PHP, and 1 PHP coins.
  • Consequences of Errors: Shortchanging a commuter triggers an angry confrontation that shakes the screen and temporarily disables steering, leaving you vulnerable to sudden roadside ambushes. Overpaying drains your nightly profits.

4. Supernatural Entities & Anomaly Detection Guide

Not everyone boarding your vehicle is human. Here is how to identify and neutralize the entities in JEEP HORROR:

1. The Doppelganger (Gaya-Gaya)

  • Visual Tell: Replicates the appearance of a commuter who previously disembarked, but with distorted finger joints, unnatural posture, or missing blink animations.
  • Counter-Tactic: Hand over their change silently without speaking or turning around. Disembark them at the earliest available stop.

2. The Headless Priest (Paring Walang Ulo)

  • Visual Tell: Appears strictly in the rear-view mirror holding a wicker basket. Looking back with the free camera shows an empty bench.
  • Counter-Tactic: Do not slam the brakes. Maintain a smooth cruise for two city blocks until church bells chime over the radio, signaling his departure.

3. The White Lady (Babaeng Nakaputi)

  • Visual Tell: A spectral floating figure seen near foggy bridges or under Balete (banyan) trees.
  • Counter-Tactic: Instantly switch headlights to low beams and extinguish interior cabin lights. High beams provoke a lethal windshield breach.

4. The Midnight Stalker

  • Visual Tell: Sits hunched in the darkest rear corner, clutching a concealed blade while ignoring fare requests.
  • Counter-Tactic: Accelerate directly toward the nearest illuminated gas station or police checkpoint to force them to flee.

5. Narrative Endings & Hidden Secrets

Your choices, driving discipline, and financial precision determine your ending:

  1. Safe Shift (True Survivor Ending):
    • Reach the central terminal depot before 04:30 AM.
    • Maintain 100% fare calculation accuracy without shortchanging passengers.
    • Safely avoid all anomaly confrontations.
  2. Lost in the Loop (Curse of the Highway Ending):
    • Hand over incorrect change three or more times and turn on high beams past the Balete tree.
    • The road signs begin repeating endlessly and your fuel gauge locks at zero.
  3. The Operator (Secret Lore Ending):
    • Tune your vintage dashboard radio precisely to 94.7 FM during the fog crossing.
    • Listen to the five-digit cipher broadcast and drive the final 2 km with parking lights only.
    • Unlocks the secret transit corporation lore cutscene.

6. Pro Survival Tips & FAQ

Can I pause or take breaks during a shift?

In JEEP HORROR, the clock continuously ticks forward during your route. You can pause the browser game, but pulling over in unlit areas between designated stations drastically increases entity ambush chances.

What should I do if the radio starts playing loud static?

Turn the radio knob once to re-tune or mute it immediately. Static masks the sound of footsteps approaching the driver’s partition from behind.
