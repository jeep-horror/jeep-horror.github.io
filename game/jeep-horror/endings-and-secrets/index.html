JEEP HORROR All Endings, Secrets & Radio Broadcast Easter Eggs

Unlock all endings in JEEP HORROR. Comprehensive guide to the True Survivor Ending, The Highway Loop, Entity Assimilation, and secret radio frequencies.

Quick Answer: JEEP HORROR features three primary endings: (1) Good Ending (Safe Shift), achieved by returning 100% accurate change and reporting all anomalies at the terminal; (2) Bad Ending (Lost in the Loop), triggered by failing three fare transactions and looking back at the White Lady; and (3) Secret Ending (The Operator), unlocked by tuning the radio to frequency 94.7 FM and completing the midnight route without headlights.

Unlocking All Narrative Endings in JEEP HORROR

Your navigational decisions, financial diligence, and psychological fortitude dictate which conclusion you receive at the end of the night.

Ending 1: Safe Shift (True Survivor)

  • Requirements:
    • Reach the terminal depot before 04:30 AM in-game time.
    • Calculate every commuter fare with 100% precision.
    • Disembark all legitimate passengers at their stated landmarks.
    • Never allow an anomaly to remain inside the vehicle for more than three stops.
  • Outcome: The sun peeks over the Manila skyline. You clock out with full earnings and receive the master driver achievement badge.

Ending 2: Lost in the Loop (Curse of the Highway)

  • Requirements:
    • Hand over incorrect change three or more times.
    • Turn high beams on while driving past the Balete tree.
    • Turn your head backward to stare directly at a doppelganger.
  • Outcome: Street signs begin repeating identically. Gas gauge remains frozen at zero, and you realize you have been driving along the same deserted turnpike indefinitely.

Ending 3: The Operator (Secret Lore Ending)

  • Requirements:
    • Locate the vintage radio dial on the right side of the dashboard.
    • Tune precisely to 94.7 FM during the fog crossing.
    • A numbers station broadcast will announce five code sequences: note down the sequence.
    • Drive the final 2 kilometers entirely on parking lights without turning on primary headlights.
  • Outcome: A hidden underground tunnel gate opens behind the bus terminal. A brief cinematic reveals the true origins of the transit entity experiments.

Hidden Easter Eggs

  • The Dashboard Rosary: Clicking the wooden rosary hanging from the center mirror three times plays a soft traditional hymn that pacifies nervous commuters for 60 seconds.
  • Driver License easter egg: Clicking the laminated driver ID on the sun visor reveals the real-life credits and thank-you notes from developer Neuroticfly Games.
