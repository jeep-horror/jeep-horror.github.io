What is JEEP HORROR?

JEEP HORROR is a critically acclaimed first-person psychological survival horror game created by independent developer Neuroticfly Games. Built on the Godot engine, the game places you directly behind the steering wheel of a traditional Philippine public jeepney during the eerie, desolate graveyard shift.

As midnight deepens, the familiar streets transform into a labyrinth of shadows. Your objective sounds simple enough: drive along designated transit routes, pick up weary night commuters, compute exact fares using your dashboard mechanical calculator, and hand back correct change. However, you quickly realize that not all passengers boarding your vehicle are human.

Uncanny entities, grotesque doppelgangers, and supernatural anomalies disguise themselves as regular passengers. To survive your shift in JEEP HORROR, you must hone your instincts, monitor your rear-view mirror relentlessly, and identify anomalies before they strike.

Core Gameplay Mechanics in JEEP HORROR

The tension in the jeep horror game stems from balancing mundane public transit duties with heart-pounding psychological dread:

1. Graveyard Shift Driving & Cabin Vigilance

Steer your jeepney along dimly lit roads, slowing down at dimly lit passenger sheds and street corners. You must manage headlight illumination, vehicle inertia, and mirror checks. Glancing in your rear-view mirror reveals the passenger cabin—where shadows stretch and unnatural movements lurk. For detailed survival tactics on your opening route, consult our comprehensive JEEP HORROR Survival Guide.

2. Dashboard Fare Calculation & Change System

Every passenger passes Philippine Peso bills or coins forward to the driver’s cabin. You must examine the passenger type (regular worker, student, senior citizen) and use your dashboard calculator to return exact change. Handing out incorrect change or hesitating too long frustrates passengers—or provokes supernatural entities waiting for a slip-up. Master every denomination and formula in our JEEP HORROR Fare & Change Guide.

3. Doppelganger & Supernatural Entity Identification

Not everyone who flags you down has a pulse. JEEP HORROR features unsettling urban legends and shapeshifting entities, including distorted doppelgangers, eerie headless figures, and spectral hitchhikers. Subtle clues—such as unblinking eyes, backwards joints, missing mirror reflections, or unsettling questions—give them away. Learn how to recognize and counteract each threat in the JEEP HORROR Entity Lore Dossier.

4. Multiple Branching Endings & Secrets

Every decision matters in JEEP HORROR: the routes you take, the passengers you allow on board, and your accuracy with fares directly influence your survival rating and story outcomes. Explore all narrative branches and hidden audio broadcasts in our JEEP HORROR Endings & Secrets Guide.

Essential Survival Tips for JEEP HORROR

  1. Never Take Your Eyes off the Rear-View Mirror: Normal passengers fidget, check their phones, or look out the window. Entities often stare directly forward with unblinking eyes.
  2. Double-Check Your Calculator Inputs: When nervousness kicks in, miscalculations happen easily. Keep mental arithmetic sharp to avoid passenger agitation.
  3. Listen Closely to Ambient Audio: Audio design in JEEP HORROR delivers vital survival cues. Static on the radio, sudden drops in engine hum, or faint whispers indicate an anomaly is nearby.
  4. Trust Your Instincts: If a passenger at a roadside shed feels fundamentally wrong, stay alert and prepare to react immediately.
