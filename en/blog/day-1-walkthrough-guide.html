JEEP HORROR Day 1 Walkthrough: How to Survive Your First Midnight Route

Your first graveyard shift in JEEP HORROR serves as your trial by fire. You'll learn to juggle vehicular movement, coin calculations on your dashboard calculator, and most crucially: maintaining unwavering vigilance over your rear-view mirror.

⚡ Quick Answer / TL;DR

To survive Day 1 in JEEP HORROR: 1) Memorize the face and attire of the first passenger (typically an overtired BPO call center agent or night-school student); 2) The moment a duplicate commuter boards with the exact same appearance, refuse their fare and immediately eject them with the Drop Off action before reaching the next intersection; 3) Swiftly count change using 1, 5, and 10 peso coin denominations to prevent the commuter satisfaction meter from collapsing before 3:00 AM.

1. Pre-Shift Terminal Setup

At shift start, your jeepney idles in a desolate transit bay. Before allowing commuters aboard, familiarize yourself with three core controls:

  • Rear-View Mirror: Click to pan your gaze backward whenever footsteps echo or when cruising into unlit highway stretches. This reveals both bench rows.
  • Fare Box & Calculator: Mounted right of the steering wheel. Commuters deposit Bayad (fare) and expect accurate Sukli (change).
  • Driver's CB Radio: Used for route dispatches and submitting formal anomaly reports.

2. Greeting the First Passenger: Voice & Glances

On Day 1, your initial commuter is normally a real civilian heading home late from an office or shopping mall. They will speak naturally and announce their stop.

Golden Rule: Real humans blink naturally, hand over legitimate currency, and react with mild annoyance if delayed. Entities that stare unblinkingly with asymmetrical grimaces or repeat corrupted audio lines must be monitored with extreme caution.

3. Neutralizing Your First Doppelganger

Around the halfway mark of Day 1's route, a commuter will board who possesses an identical model to someone already seated inside:

  1. Scan both benches via the rear-view mirror.
  2. If two individuals share identical facial geometry, clothing colors, and hair, you are dealing with a Doppelganger anomaly.
  3. The latest boarder is almost universally the counterfeit entity. Do not hesitate: trigger Drop Off immediately. Allowing a doppelganger to arrive at the stop alongside the original results in a violent cabin massacre and an instant Game Over.

4. Maintaining Passenger Satisfaction

The commuter satisfaction bar decays rapidly if:

  • You spend too long computing change on the calculator;
  • You shortchange or overcharge passengers;
  • You file spurious radio reports without verified monster evidence.
