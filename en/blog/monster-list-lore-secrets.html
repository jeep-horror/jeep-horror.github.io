JEEP HORROR All Monsters & Monster Book: Identification Guide, Kataw & Secrets

Looking for the complete list of JEEP HORROR monsters? Rooted directly in Philippine folklore and urban highway legends, this definitive game wiki breaks down every entity found in the driver's Monster Book, including the shapeshifting Kataw, cabin doppelgangers, and nocturnal serial killers.

⚡ Quick Answer / TL;DR

JEEP HORROR all monsters guide: 1) Cabin Doppelgangers: Identical clones of existing passengers — eject via Drop Off; 2) Kataw: A shapeshifting Visayan water entity disguised as a drenched commuter, leaving wet puddles on benches and paying with slippery sea coins; 3) Headless Priest (Paring Pugot): Appears near old churches, counter with the dashboard Holy Rosary; 4) White Lady of Balete Drive: Appears in the rear-view mirror — glance away within 2 seconds; 5) Monster Book: Click the driver's handbook to decode entity traits and submit accurate CB Radio reports.

1. The JEEP HORROR Monster Book: Decoding Entity Profiles

In JEEP HORROR, the Monster Book is your most critical survival tool. Kept in the driver's glove compartment, this bestiary catalogues paranormal entities, anomalous behaviors, and banishing rituals. You unlock new lore entries by surviving nights and successfully reporting strange commuter activity over the radio.

2. The JEEP HORROR Cabin Doppelganger

The most frequent threat among all JEEP HORROR monsters. This entity mimics an existing commuter down to their uniform, backpack color, and sitting posture.

  • Recognition Signs: Identical clothing, unblinking eyes, unnatural stillness.
  • Countermeasure: Immediately trigger Drop Off on the newer boarder before reaching their destination stop.

3. Jeep Horror Kataw: The Shapeshifting Water Creature

In Philippine Visayan mythology, the Kataw is a merman or aquatic shapeshifter capable of walking on land. In JEEP HORROR, the Kataw disguises itself as a stranded commuter boarding during rainstorms or coastal highway routes:

  • Visual & Audio Cues: Damp hair clinging to its face, subtle trails of dripping water on the jeepney floorboards, and a faint odor of brine or fish.
  • Currency Anomaly: The Kataw hands you coins covered in slick residue or cold condensation.
  • Survival Tactic: Do not accept wet currency! Refuse entry or drop them off before they drag other commuters into nocturnal paralysis.

4. Paring Pugot: The Headless Priest in JEEP HORROR

A fixture of Philippine ghost lore originating from the colonial and WWII eras. In-game, this apparition appears along secluded roads bordering old church plazas.

  • Recognition Signs: Flowing dark cassock with an empty collar, accompanied by distant tolling bells.
  • Countermeasure: Engage the Holy Rosary hanging from your dashboard mirror. If lacking sacred items, maintain steady velocity and refrain from slamming the emergency brake.

5. The White Lady of Balete Drive in JEEP HORROR

Dressed in a flowing white gown with midnight-black hair obscuring her countenance, she materializes as your vehicle passes beneath towering Balete (banyan) trees.

Mirror Rule: She does not appear outside your passenger window; she materializes directly in your rear-view mirror staring intently into your eyes. Divert your gaze back onto the road immediately to prevent sudden vision impairment.

6. The Concealed Cabin Killer

Not every nightmare is supernatural. Human killers will occasionally board your jeepney with the intent to rob or assassinate fellow passengers.

  1. Watch for commuters fidgeting with concealed blades beneath heavy jackets.
  2. Never engage them in direct combat: Tap the CB Radio to quietly dispatch an alert to local barangay watchpoints.

7. The Craft & Worldbuilding Behind JEEP HORROR

Developed independently using the Godot Engine, the creator explicitly confirmed: No Generative AI was used. Every passenger sprite, interior ambient rumble, and background asset was hand-crafted to capture authentic Filipino public transit culture.
