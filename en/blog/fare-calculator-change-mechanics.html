Fare Calculator & Change Mechanics in JEEP HORROR: Survival Guide

In traditional Philippine transit, every driver lives by the golden rule: "Coins only in the morning". In JEEP HORROR, miscalculating change is just as lethal as picking up an aswang or cabin doppelganger.

⚡ Quick Answer / TL;DR

To solve change rapidly in JEEP HORROR: Standard fare is typically ₱13 (regular) or ₱10 (discounted). When a passenger hands over ₱20, promptly input ₱7 change using one ₱5 coin and two ₱1 coins; when given ₱50, dispense ₱37. Beware of anomalous currency with bloodstains or archaic colonial mint marks — accepting cursed money directly invites supernatural entity attacks inside your vehicle.

1. The JEEP HORROR Commuter Fare Loop: "Bayad po!"

Every few minutes along the midnight route, a commuter calls out "Bayad po!" (Here is my fare!). Currency lands onto the wooden coin tray mounted adjacent to your steering wheel.

Click the handed money to open the Fare Calculator display. This HUD shows:

  • Amount Handed: ₱20, ₱50 bills, or miscellaneous coins.
  • Fare Required: Computed from passenger type and route distance.
  • Coin Tray Denominations: Slots containing ₱1, ₱5, ₱10, and ₱20 coins.

2. Optimal Denomination Assembly

Never waste precious seconds clicking endless ₱1 coins for large transactions. Always assemble change from the highest denomination downward:

  1. Example: ₱17 change = One ₱10 + One ₱5 + Two ₱1.
  2. Example: ₱8 change = One ₱5 + Three ₱1.

Once your counter matches the target change amount, press Return Change to return to driving mode.

3. Why Commuter Satisfaction Plummets in JEEP HORROR

Shortchanging passengers immediately triggers furious verbal disputes inside the cabin. Aside from reducing your shift satisfaction bar, loud arguments attract predators roaming the roadside shadows.

Anomalous Currency Warning: If a passenger hands you charred currency, antebellum coins, or notes dripping damp liquid, do not add them to your till! Click Reject Tender and immediately inspect the passenger via the rear-view mirror.

4. JEEP HORROR Speed Tips for High Scores

  • Mentally calculate Tender - Fare = Change before the calculator dialog fully renders.
  • Complete change calculations in under 5 seconds to earn a "Speed Tip" bonus score.
  • Keep small coin reserves balanced before hitting the congested 2:00 AM rush.
