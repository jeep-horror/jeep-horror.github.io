Core Mechanics

Why Every Midnight Passenger Puts You on Edge

A claustrophobic fusion of authentic Filipino public transit culture and psychological survival horror.

👥

Cabin Doppelgangers

Malevolent entities will perfectly mimic another passenger already seated behind you. If you fail to spot the duplicate face and let them step off, your route ends permanently.

💰

Fare Calculator & Exact Change

"Coins only in the morning!" Computing change with 1, 5, and 10 peso coins in pitch darkness is frantic. Too slow or giving incorrect change tanks commuter satisfaction.

📻

Monster Book & Radio Alert

Consult the driver's logbook to decode hints on mythological horrors like the Headless Priest, Balete Drive apparitions, and disguised back-seat serial killers.
